* [¿Es posible obtener estimaciones confiables del porcentaje de anemia y retraso en el crecimiento en niños menores de cinco años en los distritos más pobres del Perú? ](https://doi.org/10.21678/apuntes.95.1811){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} <br> 
with [Anna Sikov] and [José Javier Cerda Hernández]{:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}  <br> 
  <button onclick="myFunction('abstract_1')" class="btn--research">Abstract</button> 
  <p id="abstract_1" style="display: none; text-align: justify; width: 100%;"><font size="2.5"> In this article we describe and apply the Fay-Herriot model with spatially correlated random area effects (Pratesi, M., & Salvati, N. (2008)), in order to predict the prevalence of anemia and childhood stunting in Peruvian districts, based on the data from the Demographic and Family Health Survey of the year 2019, which collects data about anemia and childhood stunting for children under the age of 12 years, and the Natio- nal Census carried out in 2017. Our main objective is to produce reliable predictions for the districts, where sample sizes are too small to provide good direct estimates, and for the districts, which were not included in the sample. </font> </p>

## Working Papers

* [Evaluación de la efectividad del subsidio “Recuperemos el Empleo Formal” en Perú](https://publications.iadb.org/es/evaluacion-de-la-efectividad-del-subsidio-recuperemos-el-empleo-formal-en-peru){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} <br> 
with [Rafael Novella](https://sites.google.com/site/rafaelnovella/) and [David Rosas-Shady] {:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}  <br> 
  <button onclick="myFunction('abstract_2')" class="btn--research">Abstract</button> 
  <p id="abstract_2" style="display: none; text-align: justify; width: 100%;"><font size="2.5"> This document presents the impact evaluation of the wage subsidy program *“Recuperemos el Empleo Formal”*, implemented between November 2020 and April 2022 to mitigate the impact of the pandemic on the labor market in Peru. The program aimed to incentivize the formal hiring of workers and preserve formal jobs. The evaluation uses quasi-experimental methodologies (entropy balancing and difference-in-differences), as well as administrative data from *Planilla Electrónica* and program implementation records. The results suggest that the program succeeded in incentivizing formal hiring, primarily of adult workers and those with temporary contracts. </font> </p> 
  <span style="color:Red; text-align: justify; font-size: 11px; display: inline-block; width: 100%;"> R&R at Journal of Money, Credit and Banking  </span>
